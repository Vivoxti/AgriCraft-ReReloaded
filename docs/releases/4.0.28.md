## AgriCraft Re-Reloaded 1.21.1-4.0.28

### Added

- Create: Integrated Farming compatibility (NeoForge, Create: Integrated Farming 1.3.0+). The Vacuum Harvester now harvests AgriCraft crops, both as a placed block and mounted on a contraption. Before, it ignored AgriCraft crops entirely.
  - Works on crops on crop sticks and on crops planted directly.
  - Mature plants give their normal genetic harvest products and return to the species' post-harvest stage.
  - Unripe plants, crop sticks and empty sticks are left untouched.

### Fixed

- Harvesting, clipping or raking no longer crashes the server when a plant or weed product points to an empty item tag or an unknown item. This happened, for example, in a modpack that emptied `#agricraft:nuggets/copper` while unifying nuggets.
  - That product is skipped and the rest of the harvest works normally.
  - AgriCraft logs one warning naming the empty tag, so pack authors can find it.

### Downloads

- `AgriCraft-neoforge-1.21.1-4.0.28.jar`: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.
- `AgriCraft-fabric-1.21.1-4.0.28.jar`: Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric.

### Validation

Both production builds and archive checks passed. A NeoForge client with Create 6.0.10 and Create: Integrated Farming 1.3.2 ran a stationary Vacuum Harvester over AgriCraft wheat with and without crop sticks. Mature crops were harvested and reset, unripe crops and sticks were untouched, and vanilla wheat was still harvested. The empty-tag crash was reproduced on 4.0.27 and did not occur with the fix. The Create Mechanical Harvester behaved the same as in 4.0.26. See [release verification](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/blob/4.0.28/docs/releases/4.0.28-VALIDATION.md) for details and limits.

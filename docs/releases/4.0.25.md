## AgriCraft Re-Reloaded 1.21.1-4.0.25

### Fixed

- AgriCraft seeds can be fed to animals again. A seed now counts as its plant's vanilla seed item for every animal food and tempt tag: chickens breed on wheat, beetroot, melon, pumpkin and torchflower seeds, parrots can be tamed, and sniffers, pigs, rabbits, foxes, pandas, turtles, camels, striders and hoglins accept their matching seeds and follow players holding them.
- Villager farming tags, recipes and other item tags still see the AgriCraft seed item. AgriCraft flower seeds have no vanilla seed item and are not treated as flowers.
- The seed bag stores seeds again instead of deleting them. Inserting seeds, taking them out, planting from the bag and changing its sorter are now saved on the bag. Identical seeds share one entry, a full stack is released at most once per click, and an emptied bag accepts a new species.
- Sneak + scroll with a seed bag in the main hand changes its sorter again, on both NeoForge and Fabric. The server stores the choice and shows it in the action bar. All seven sorters (overall quality and one per stat) are available from the first use.
- `/agricraft_seed` no longer disconnects the player after giving the seed. Its chat reply now names the plant instead of sending a raw identifier.

### Downloads

- `AgriCraft-neoforge-1.21.1-4.0.25.jar` — Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.
- `AgriCraft-fabric-1.21.1-4.0.25.jar` — Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric.

### Validation

Both production builds and archive checks passed. Regression probes for animal food, the seed bag, sorter switching and command messages passed on NeoForge development servers, and the seed bag and sorter scrolling were checked by hand in a NeoForge client. Fabric passed build, archive and mixin remapping checks and started to the main menu in a development client; no Fabric gameplay session was run. See [release verification](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/blob/4.0.25/docs/releases/4.0.25-VALIDATION.md) for details and limits.

## AgriCraft Re-Reloaded 1.21.1-4.0.23

### Fixed

- **NeoForge:** AgriCraft seeds can be composted again by right-clicking a composter or inserting them through a hopper.
- Composting respects `seed_compost_value`: the default is `0.3`, and `0` disables composting.
- The configured compost chance remains effective after `/reload`.

### Downloads

- `AgriCraft-neoforge-1.21.1-4.0.23.jar` — Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.
- `AgriCraft-fabric-1.21.1-4.0.23.jar` — Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric. The composting fix applies to NeoForge; Fabric retains its existing implementation.

### Validation

Both production builds and archive checks passed. The final NeoForge JAR passed 98 dedicated-server checks covering configured chances of `0`, `0.3` and `1`, player interactions, hopper insertion and resource reloads. The local fix also passed the maintainer's manual client check. Fabric passed build and archive validation; a Fabric gameplay session was not run. See [release verification](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/blob/4.0.23/docs/releases/4.0.23-VALIDATION.md) for evidence and limits.

## AgriCraft Re-Reloaded 1.21.1-4.0.29

### Changed

- The Gain stat now improves the Plant Clipper ([#8](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/issues/8)). Before, Gain had no effect on clipping, and a vanilla flower gave its flower only about one clip in four, so the clipper looked unreliable.
  - When a clipping roll yields nothing, Gain gives it a second chance of `(Gain − 1) / 9`.
  - Gain 1 keeps the plant's own chance, Gain 10 always yields at least one item.
  - The crop still returns to its first growth stage after every clip, whether or not anything dropped.

| Gain | Chance that clipping a vanilla flower gives the flower |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 25% (unchanged) |
| 4 | 50% |
| 7 | 75% |
| 10 | 100% |

### Added

- New option `core.clipping_uses_gain` (default `true`). Set it to `false` to restore the original AgriCraft behaviour, where Gain does not affect clipping.

### Downloads

- `AgriCraft-neoforge-1.21.1-4.0.29.jar`: Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.
- `AgriCraft-fabric-1.21.1-4.0.29.jar`: Minecraft 1.21.1 with Fabric.

### Validation

Both production builds and archive checks passed. In a NeoForge client, an automated probe clipped a mature poppy 400 times at each Gain level: Gain 1, 4, 7 and 10 gave the flower in about 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of clips, and Gain 10 with the option turned off fell back to about 25%. Every clip reset the crop. See [release verification](https://github.com/Vivoxti/AgriCraft-ReReloaded/blob/4.0.29/docs/releases/4.0.29-VALIDATION.md) for details and limits.
